The midge season that ruined a perfectly good weekend

By Pat Ridge — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 22:19:57 GMT

Locals treat the midge season as a rite of passage, or a warning.

Every guidebook mentions the midge season and none of them explain it.

It rained sideways, then stopped, then reconsidered.

The map was right.

The path was not.

We turned back at four, which everyone agreed was sensible and nobody was happy about.
