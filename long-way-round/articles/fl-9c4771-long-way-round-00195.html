Three days out, and the river crossing won — offer

By Pat Ridge — Sat, 01 Aug 2026 02:02:31 GMT

It rained sideways, then stopped, then reconsidered.

The map was right.

The path was not.

Every guidebook mentions the river crossing and none of them explain it.

Opinion remains split on whether wet feet management counts as a hobby or a grievance.

We turned back at four, which everyone agreed was sensible and nobody was happy about.

The forecast promised sun; wet feet management promised otherwise and was correct.
